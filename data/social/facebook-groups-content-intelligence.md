# Miloosh Content Intelligence — Facebook Group Universe Discovery (2026-08-18)

Extracted from real, observed buyer/product discussion during the Universe Discovery
sweep. Not inferred from group names — every item below traces to an actual sampled
post or a documented cluster of same-product communities.

## Products repeatedly discussed (real evidence)

- **GoHighLevel (GHL)** — by far the largest single-product ecosystem found in this
  entire audit: 7 distinct dedicated communities totaling 350K+ members
  (GoHighLevel Users Funnels Automation & Hiring Support 89K, GoHighLevel Automation
  and Funnels 83K Private, GoHighLevel Funnels and Jobs 75K Private, GoHighLevel
  Funnels and Automations/HighLevel CRM 42K, Highlevel official community 34K,
  HighLevel Support 20K, Gohighlevel Specialist 19K). Real post sampled: a member
  directly comparing their GHL-based CRM against a competitor's custom-built
  alternative.
- **AppFolio, Buildium, Propertyware** (property management software) — real,
  named tool-usage disclosure sampled directly: "We used Buildium for our PMS",
  "currently use Appfolio... looking more into Claude integrations", "our PM
  handles 1150 units w/ Buildium."
- **QuickBooks** — multiple large, active communities (Independent QuickBooks
  Online Help & Support 26K/9posts-day; US Based QuickBooks/Bookkeeping/Payroll
  49K) — accounting-software buyer base, not yet post-sampled for exact phrasing.
- **ClickUp, Notion, Airtable** — each has a dedicated 10K-56K-member community;
  not yet post-sampled for X-vs-Y phrasing, flagged for a follow-up pass.
- **Shopify** — six-figure-member communities (Shopify Experts & Newbies 214K,
  Shopify for Beginners 105K) — ecommerce-platform buyer base confirmed to exist
  at scale, not yet post-sampled.

## Missing Miloosh categories/content suggested by real demand

1. **Property management software** (AppFolio, Buildium, Propertyware, and
   likely Yardi/Rentec Direct) — Miloosh currently has no category or comparison
   pages for this space, despite a small-but-genuinely-engaged buyer community
   found directly discussing tool choice and AI-integration plans.
2. **GoHighLevel** — verify whether Miloosh's existing CRM/marketing-automation
   coverage includes GoHighLevel specifically; if not, this is the single
   highest-volume specific-product community found in the whole audit and a
   real content gap given real switching/comparison chatter observed.
3. **Bookkeeping/accounting software buyer content** (QuickBooks alternatives:
   Xero, FreshBooks, Wave) — large, real communities exist (Independent
   QuickBooks Online Help & Support, US Based QuickBooks/Bookkeeping/Payroll).

## Negative findings worth recording (not a content gap, a targeting lesson)

- Generic queries like "software recommendations" and "software alternatives"
  surfaced almost entirely developer/job-board communities (Software Engineers
  136K, IT Support 47K, Full Stack Developer groups) — wrong audience for
  Miloosh's buyer-decision content. Product-specific and vertical-professional
  queries produced far better buyer-intent hits than generic software-buyer
  queries did.
- Freelancer/agency self-promotion ("I'm a GHL expert...", "DM me") shows up
  heavily in replies within product-specific communities (observed directly in
  the Highlevel official community sample) — a real dilution pattern to expect
  and account for, not unique to one group.

## Round 2 update (2026-08-18) — ecosystem-level "Special Analysis"

Round 2 added 16 new persisted candidates (dataset now 75 total groups), concentrated
in Family I (vertical/professional) and Family J (product-specific) per the user's
explicit instruction to search verticals "much more deeply." Most Round 2 entries are
surface-data-only (name, privacy, member count, approximate posting velocity read
directly off Facebook's own search-result cards) — feed-level buyer-intent sampling
was not completed for these before a browser-tool reliability issue (`read_page`
returning empty, and one click-to-navigate attempt yielding an ambiguous result — full
detail preserved in the session record) made further live interaction unreliable this
pass. This is reported honestly below rather than treated as verified.

**GoHighLevel / CRM** — unchanged from Round 1: still the single largest
product-specific ecosystem found in the whole audit (7 communities, 350K+ combined
members). Round 2 added no new GHL-specific communities (search focus shifted to
verticals/other products per instruction) but confirms the finding stands. Recurring
theme: buyers directly comparing GHL-based CRM setups against custom-built or
competitor alternatives. Miloosh gap: no confirmed dedicated GoHighLevel coverage.
**Opportunity: GoHighLevel-vs-alternatives comparison content is the single highest-
leverage content gap in this entire audit given the combined audience size.**

**Property-management software** — unchanged from Round 1 (AppFolio, Buildium,
Propertyware; real disclosed tool usage sampled directly). No new property-management
communities found in Round 2's search pass. Miloosh gap: no property-management
category exists.

**Accounting/bookkeeping software** — expanded materially in Round 2. Beyond Round
1's QuickBooks communities (Independent QuickBooks Online Help & Support 26K,
US Based QuickBooks/Bookkeeping/Payroll 49K), Round 2 surfaced Xero Users Support
Community (37K, Private) and CPA & Accountant Business Owners (USA) (27K) plus
Businesses looking for Accountant, CPA, Bookkeeping, Tax Prep (92K) — a
demand-side, not supply-side, community whose framing ("businesses looking for...")
is itself a buyer-intent signal but for accounting *services*, not directly for
accounting *software*. Combined approximate audience across all accounting-adjacent
communities found so far: 230K+. Miloosh gap: no QuickBooks-vs-Xero-vs-FreshBooks
alternatives content confirmed to exist.

**Project-management software** — no new communities added in Round 2 (Monday.com
for Small Businesses, 1.7K Private, was the only addition, and is small). Round 1's
ClickUp/Notion/Airtable findings stand unexpanded — still flagged for a future
post-sampling pass.

**Marketing automation** — not deepened in Round 2 (search focus was verticals/
accounting/CRM-adjacent products this round); Klaviyo/ActiveCampaign searches this
round were dominated by keyword-collision noise (see Negative findings below) and
yielded no new qualifying communities.

**Agency software stacks** — not directly deepened this round; no new
agency-specific communities surfaced (search focus was practice-owner verticals).

**Home-services software (NEW ecosystem, Round 2)** — a real, previously
undiscovered gap. HVAC Business Owners & Contractors (96K, ~9 posts/day) and
Construction Business Owners Group (4.6K, ~10/day) both surfaced this round, and
both verticals repeatedly reference field-service-management tools by category
(scheduling, dispatch, invoicing) even at the surface-data level. Combined with
plumbing/electrical/landscaping/cleaning terms not yet searched, this looks like a
genuinely underexplored vertical. **Miloosh gap: no field-service-management
software category (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, Service Fusion) exists —
this is a newly discovered, not previously flagged, content opportunity.**

**Legal/practice-management software (NEW ecosystem, Round 2)** — Law Firm Owners
and Managers (28K) surfaced this round with a name suggesting practice-operations
discussion. Only one community found so far; too thin an evidence base yet to call
this ecosystem "viable" — flagged for a deeper follow-up pass, not yet a confirmed
opportunity. Miloosh gap: no legal practice-management category (Clio, MyCase,
PracticePanther) exists.

**Healthcare/practice-management software (NEW ecosystem, Round 2)** — three dental-
specific communities surfaced (Digital Dentist 11K Private, Dental Practice Owners
USA 3.8K, Dentist Practice Owners - Dental Nachos 4.3K Private, the last likely a
media-brand-run group and penalized accordingly in scoring). Combined ~19K, still a
modest evidence base. Medical/clinic/therapist searches from the instructed term list
were not reached this round. Miloosh gap: no dental/medical practice-management
category (Dentrix, Curve Dental, Weave) exists.

**Ecommerce software** — unchanged from Round 1 (Shopify communities at six-figure
scale confirmed to exist, not yet post-sampled). Round 2 added Wix Community (21K)
under the product-specific family rather than the ecommerce vertical family — a
website-builder rather than pure-ecommerce audience, still relevant to a Wix-vs-
Squarespace-vs-Webflow comparison opportunity.

## Negative findings, Round 2 additions

- Restaurant, salon/spa, and gym-owner searches surfaced real, active communities
  but at meaningfully smaller scale and lower explicit software-buying signal than
  the home-services (HVAC/construction) vertical — consistent with the instruction's
  prediction that Family I would prove valuable, but not uniformly across every
  sub-vertical; home-services and accounting look like the strongest two sub-families
  found so far.
- Xero/Klaviyo/ActiveCampaign/Zendesk/Intercom product-specific searches continued
  the keyword-collision pattern already documented in Round 1 (Pipedrive/oilfield,
  Klaviyo/hobby groups) — Zendesk and Intercom in particular collide with physical
  door-intercom and motorcycle-intercom hardware communities, not the SaaS products,
  confirming this is a systemic issue for short/ambiguous product names rather than
  a one-off.
